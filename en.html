About

A Qatari fund, an African conviction

Black Ventures Capital is an investment fund dedicated exclusively to development projects in Africa. We mobilise patient capital and structuring expertise to turn ambitious projects into concrete results.

Our role doesn't stop at financing: we support governments in structuring their major infrastructure and public-service projects, and we help the private sector build sound, innovative financial arrangements suited to local realities.

Our conviction is simple: Africa has the resources, the markets and the human capital needed for its transformation. What is often missing is a partner able to structure, finance and stay engaged over the long term.

Advisory & financial structuring

A structuring partner, not just a source of funds

We get involved upstream of financing, to build bankable projects and durable arrangements.

For governments

Structuring public infrastructure projects, public-private partnership arrangements, support in mobilising international financing and dialogue with lenders.

For the private sector

Financial structuring of industrial and agricultural projects, debt and equity arrangements, access to alternative financing instruments suited to each market.

Financial structuring

Designing arrangements that combine capital, concessional debt and guarantees, to optimise the cost of capital and share risk among stakeholders.

Long-term support

Implementation follow-up, project governance and impact reporting, from financial close through to operation.

Our approach

From idea to funded project

A three-step method, applied to every project we support.

01

Diagnosis & structuring

We assess the technical, economic and financial feasibility of the project, and design an arrangement suited to its context and sponsors.

02

Capital mobilisation & partnerships

We commit our own resources and bring in complementary financial partners to secure the project's financial close.

03

Monitoring, governance & impact

We support the project's delivery over time, with rigorous monitoring of its performance and impact on the territories involved.
